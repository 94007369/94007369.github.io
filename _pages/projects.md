## Sustaining Juneau: A Policy-Driven Approach to Tourism Resilience
<p class="meta">Team Leader · Mathematical Contest in Modeling · January 2025 · Meritorious Winner</p>

Used **Python** to collect, clean, and visualize policy and tourism data released by the Juneau municipal government. Applied **nonlinear regression, logistic regression, and the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)** to evaluate policy trade-offs and construct a sustainability score. Authored and edited the team's technical report in **LaTeX**.

## AI Governance: Dataset Construction & Measurement

## Carbon Trading: Policy Text Analysis
