I use quantitative and qualitative approaches to study governance, institutions, and the political economy of technology.

## Selected research projects

<h3 id="ai-governance">Measuring Private-Sector Embeddedness in U.S. AI Governance</h3>
<p class="meta">Final research project · POLISCI 157, Stanford Summer Session · Summer 2026</p>

Developed **Private-Sector Role Policy Embeddedness (PRPE)**, an original actor-level measure capturing the seniority, recency, and policy relevance of government officials' prior private-sector ties. Built and verified a dataset of senior U.S. AI-governance officials and compared industry-government embeddedness across the Biden and second Trump administrations.

**Methods:** Dataset construction and verification; actor-level measurement; comparative quantitative analysis.

<h3 id="carbon-trading">Cross-Regional Digital Collaborative Capacity in China's Carbon Trading Market</h3>
<p class="meta">Fudan University · Presented at the FDU-ECNU-SJTU Academic Forum · May 2025</p>

Analyzed policy documents using **TF-IDF** and **Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)** to explore cross-regional digital collaborative capacity. Conducted interviews with enterprises and the Shanghai Environment and Energy Exchange to contextualize and validate the text-based findings.

**Methods:** Computational text analysis; topic modeling; policy document analysis; semi-structured interviews. **Recognition:** Best Paper Award, FDU-ECNU-SJTU Academic Forum.
